FAQ

Common questions, simple answers.

Which classes are covered?

Tuition enquiries are currently intended for Classes 1–10.

Is tuition available online?

Yes. Online tuition can support students in Telangana, other parts of India and potentially international learners depending on the requirement.

Is home tuition available?

Home tuition enquiries can be shared for your location. Availability depends on the area and suitable tuition options.

Do I need to know the exact subject?

No. If you are unsure, simply tell us what support the learner needs.

Do I need to fill every field?

No. Provide whatever information you already know. The remaining details can be discussed through WhatsApp.

Is there pricing shown on the website?

No fixed pricing is displayed here. Tuition requirements can differ, so details can be discussed after the enquiry.

Is the demo class free?

You can use the Free Demo Class option to share the learner's requirements and discuss the available demo arrangement through WhatsApp.
