FAQ

Common questions.

Still have a question? Send us an inquiry and tell us what you are looking for.

Do you provide online tuition? +

Yes. Online tuition can be explored for students who want to learn remotely, including students outside the immediate local area.

Do you provide home tuition? +

Home tuition inquiries can be made for your location. Availability depends on suitable teacher availability.

Which classes are covered? +

Tuition support is intended for students from Classes 1 through 10.

Which subjects are available? +

You can inquire about Mathematics, Science, English, Social Studies, multiple subjects and other academic requirements.

Can students outside Telangana inquire? +

Yes. Online tuition can make it possible to connect with students beyond the local area. International students can also submit an inquiry for online learning.

Do I need to choose a tuition option before contacting you? +

No. You can simply make an inquiry and explain what you need. We can start from there.
