How to Plan a Good Tuition Session

A practical session-planning framework for online, home and one-to-one tutoring.

Find a TutorBecome a Tutor

A simple plan is better than an overloaded plan

A tuition session should have a clear purpose while leaving enough flexibility to respond to the student's actual understanding. Planning is not about predicting every minute; it is about knowing what matters most.

A practical five-part structure

  1. Warm-up: briefly revisit the previous learning.
  2. Goal: state what the student will work toward today.
  3. Teach: introduce or clarify the concept.
  4. Practise: let the student apply it with guidance.
  5. Review: check understanding and identify the next step.

Plan for different learning levels

Two students in the same class may need different amounts of support. Prepare an easier starting example and a slightly more challenging extension when appropriate. This allows the session to adapt without making the student feel singled out.

Online session planning

Check links, files and devices before the session when possible. Keep digital materials organised and have a backup explanation if a tool fails. The lesson should still work even if a particular technology is unavailable.

Home tuition planning

Know what materials are needed before arriving. Clarify the expected session duration and learning goal. Keep the environment professional and avoid unnecessary disruption to the household.

After the session

Make a short note of what was covered, what the student understood, what needs reinforcement and what should happen next. This creates continuity without requiring complicated administration.
