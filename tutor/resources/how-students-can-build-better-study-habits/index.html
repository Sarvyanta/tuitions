How Students Can Build Better Study Habits

Practical study habits for school students learning online, at home or in a classroom environment.

Find a TutorBecome a Tutor

Good study habits are built gradually

Studying effectively is not about sitting with books for the maximum number of hours. It is about focused practice, understanding, review and consistency.

Start with a small, clear goal

Instead of “I will study science,” choose a specific task such as reviewing one concept, solving a set of problems or explaining a topic without looking at the notes.

Use active learning

  • Try to recall information before looking at the answer.
  • Solve problems instead of only reading worked examples.
  • Explain a concept in your own words.
  • Use mistakes to identify what needs more practice.

Manage distractions

Keep unnecessary notifications and unrelated apps away during focused study. Online learners should take particular care because the same device used for learning can also provide many distractions.

Make questions part of learning

If something is unclear, write down the question and ask the teacher, tutor or another appropriate source. Asking a precise question is a useful learning skill.

Review over time

Short reviews spread across several days can help maintain understanding better than trying to learn everything immediately before an assessment. Combine review with practice rather than repeatedly reading the same page.

Look after the basics

Sleep, breaks, movement, hydration and a reasonable routine support the ability to concentrate. If a student is consistently exhausted, overwhelmed or unable to cope, talk to a parent, teacher or appropriate professional rather than simply adding more study time.
